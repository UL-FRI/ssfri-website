We are looking to recruit a junior researchers at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science, University of Ljubljana, Slovenia in the area of mobile wireless network data collection and analysis. The position is offered within a European H2020 project.

You will join an existing team of wireless networking and data mining experts in Ljubljana and work on setting up and maintaining software and hardware for local wireless measurements, conducting experiments in both local and remote MONROE mobile wireless testbed, and, together with the data mining experts, analyse the data and provide feedback to the MONROE project partners.

You should have at least a bachelors degree in computer science, computer engineering or electrical engineering. You should have strong background in Linux programming and scripting languages. Previous experience with Docker,  distributed experimentation and wireless communication protocols is a plus.

Types of employment: full-time or part-time one-year contract, student work.

If you are interested to apply, send a detailed CV by February 20th to <veljko.pejovic@fri.uni-lj.si>

For further information contact prof. Veljko Pejovic <veljko.pejovic@fri.uni-lj.si>
