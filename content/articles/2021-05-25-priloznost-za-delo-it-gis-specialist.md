The FH Kärnten (Carinthia University of Applied Sciences, Austria) invites applications for a  fully-funded BSc position in the area of a platform for big spatial data management for  semantic integration.

**Your responsibilities **

The KWF “Umsetzung innovativer Gründungsvorhaben” 2021 (UiG) project will be  supervised by Assoc. Prof. Tomaž Podobnikar. It will be focused to design and develop a  prototype platform for the implementation of spatial data conflation with services, plug-ins or  similar. The methods used will depend on the final focus that will be developed during the  project, ranging from C++, Java, PHP, Python, to backend SQL, ArcGIS Server, Geoserver,  PostGIS, as well as frontend Openlayers, Mapbox or similar.

**Your profile **

We are looking for a highly motivated fellow with a passion for innovation, and with an  independent, efficient and effective work style. Applicants must have completed a BSc degree  or close to achieving it or have relevant professional experience in Computer Science,  GIScience, Data Science, or a related field. A strong background in programming is a  prerequisite. Ideally, the candidate also has experience in machine/deep learning and  ontological models. The successful applicant will also have a profound interest in  collaborating within an interdisciplinary team. Decent spoken and written English  communication skills are mandatory. Knowledge of German is considered an asset.

**Start of employment and duration **

August 1, 2021; 9 months

**Further information and application **

For informal inquiries contact Assoc. Prof. Tomaž Podobnikar. Please apply at  tomaz.podobnikar1@guest.arnes.si the following documents:

1\) Cover letter emphasizing motivation to apply, incl. preferred focus areas (1 page max.)

2\) Curriculum vitae, including if available, access information to repositories that showcase  your programming in an academic context, for example your GitHub portfolio (2 pages max.)

3\) Academic degrees\
